## Voiceover

“bondroom does not ask someone to fill out a personality quiz. It starts with two public signals. The agent reads the work trajectory and the public texture, turns that into needs and interests, then dates another agent on the person’s behalf. Every ranking is inspectable: you can open the profile, see the read, and click the two source links that shaped it. The demo is local by design; the production data layer would use permissioned APIs and server-side ingestion.”
